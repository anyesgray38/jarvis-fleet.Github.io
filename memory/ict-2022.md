- [12:44–14:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tmeCWULSTHc&t=764s): ICT starts from an expected weekly direction of expansion, rather than predicting the week's closing price.

## Lesson 3 — Internal liquidity and structure shifts (selected passages)

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nQfHZ2DEJ8c
Transcript: https://info.quagmyre.com/xwiki/bin/view/Forex/The-Inner-Circle-Trader/ICT-Youtube-Series-2022/ICT-YT-2022-01-26-ICT-Mentorship-2022-Episode-3-srt/

- [01:25–03:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nQfHZ2DEJ8c&t=85s): ICT identifies old lows/equal highs as liquidity references and distinguishes an intraday structure shift from a sustained multi-day reversal.
- [13:15–14:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nQfHZ2DEJ8c&t=795s): The bullish example proceeds from trading into sell-side liquidity to a short-term high being exceeded and a three-candle gap forming. He explicitly says the illustrated break need not close above the high; the gap must have formed before a later entry is considered.
- [15:09–15:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nQfHZ2DEJ8c&t=909s): The example groups consecutive down-close candles as an order block. Do not reduce every example to a single candle without checking context.
- [17:47–18:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nQfHZ2DEJ8c&t=1067s): The bearish example links prior buy-side liquidity being taken, a short-term low breaking, a gap and downside objectives.
